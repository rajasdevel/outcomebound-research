# MiniMax Speech 2.8 Turbo

MiniMax Speech 2.8 Turbo is a speech synthesis model of the MiniMax Speech Turbo class, generation 2.8. It plays human sounds from inline tags in the text, takes an emotion field and has voice design from a text description. MiniMax released it on 2026-01-23. It is a tag-directed model. It has no free-text instruction field. Family-wide rules are in the [MiniMax README](README.md).

## How to instruct it

### Setting up the prompt

- **No system prompt.** The input is `text` with optional markup, plus `voice_setting`, `audio_setting` and related objects. The model id is `speech-2.8-turbo` [mm-t2a-http].
- **Controls.** An emotion field, parenthesised interjection tags, speed, volume and pitch, and `voice_modify` effects. Details are in the voice section.
- **HD and Turbo.** MiniMax lists both with the same capabilities. HD is the higher-quality model, and Turbo is tuned for speed and cost [mm-ws-guide] [mm-paygo]. The full account is in the [HD file](speech-2.8-hd.md). Turbo takes the same tags, emotion values and settings.

### Reasoning and effort

None.

### Output: format, length, tone

- **Formats.** See the card. The default is mp3 [mm-t2a-http].
- **Length.** See the card [mm-t2a-http] [mm-async].
- **Tone.** Set by the voice, the emotion field, interjection tags and the text [mm-t2a-http].
- **Region.** MiniMax recommends `api-uw.minimax.io` for US West, for a lower time to first audio [mm-ws-guide].

### Tools and agents

Not applicable.

### Coding

The pages read give no coding guidance for the speech models.

### Long context and retrieval

Long text uses streaming (above 3,000 characters) or the asynchronous endpoint (up to 1M characters) [mm-t2a-http] [mm-async].

### Structured output

Not applicable.

### Images, audio, other inputs

Text only. A cloned voice needs a short audio sample [mm-voice-clone].

### Voice: turn-taking, interruption and speech style

MiniMax Speech 2.8 is not a conversation model. It has no turn detection and no barge-in. A client stops its own playback.

**How to direct delivery.** MiniMax has no free-text instruction field. It has three controls: an emotion field, inline interjection tags, and voice settings.

- **Interjection tags (2.8 only).** The text carries tags in parentheses that play a human sound at that point: `(laughs)`, `(chuckle)`, `(coughs)`, `(clear-throat)`, `(groans)`, `(breath)`, `(pant)`, `(inhale)`, `(exhale)`, `(gasps)`, `(sniffs)`, `(sighs)`, `(snorts)`, `(burps)`, `(lip-smacking)`, `(humming)`, `(hissing)`, `(emm)` and `(sneezes)`. The API reference marks them as supported on the 2.8 models only. The launch page for 2.8 describes "native sound tags" that model fillers such as um and uh [mm-t2a-http] [mm-news-28].
- **Emotion field.** `voice_setting.emotion` takes `happy`, `sad`, `angry`, `fearful`, `disgusted`, `surprised` or `calm` on 2.8. `fluent` and `whisper` exist on Speech 2.6 only. By default the model picks the emotion that fits the text, and the reference advises a manual value only when it is explicitly needed [mm-t2a-http].
- **Illustration, written for this library and not taken from the maker.** Text: `That was the last bus (sighs). We walk, then.` with `emotion` set to `sad`. This shows the syntax; it is not a sample from the reference. It was not run against the model.
- **Speed, volume and pitch.** `speed` 0.5 to 2, `vol` above 0 up to 10, `pitch` from -12 to 12 [mm-t2a-http].
- **Voice effects.** `voice_modify` has `pitch`, `intensity` and `timbre` sliders from -100 to 100, and `sound_effects` such as a spacious echo. It works on non-streaming mp3, wav and flac, and on streaming mp3 [mm-t2a-http].
- **Pauses.** A marker of the form `<#0.8#>` between speakable text gives a pause of 0.01 to 99.99 seconds. Markers cannot be used one after another [mm-t2a-http].
- **Strength and limits.** MiniMax gives no figure for how strongly the emotion field or the tags act. The API reference says the model chooses an emotion automatically, which is why a manual value is rarely needed. Interjection tags are absent from 2.6 [mm-t2a-http].

**Voice design and cloning.**

- **Design.** `voice-design` takes a description (`prompt`) and a `preview_text` and returns a `voice_id` that works in synchronous and asynchronous synthesis. The designed voice is charged at first use [mm-voice-design] [mm-paygo].
- **Cloning.** Rapid cloning takes a sample of at least 10 seconds and at most 5 minutes; the launch page says 10 seconds is enough. A cloned voice that is not used within 7 days is deleted [mm-voice-clone] [mm-news-28].
- **Mixing.** `timbre_weights` mixes up to four voices with weights from 1 to 100. The reference marks the field legacy. It mixes voices; it is not a dialogue feature [mm-t2a-http].

**Multi-speaker dialogue and long-form consistency.** The pages read describe no multi-speaker request. Each request uses one voice. Text over 3,000 characters is advised as a stream, and paragraph breaks are newline characters. Asynchronous jobs take up to 1M characters [mm-t2a-http] [mm-async].

**Pronunciation and text normalisation.** Inline respelling in half-width parentheses: Mandarin Pinyin with a tone number, IPA, or Cantonese Jyutping. A `pronunciation_dict` of "original/replacement" rules also takes Japanese kana. `text_normalization` (Chinese and English) improves digit reading and adds a little latency; it defaults to off. `language_boost` raises recognition of a named language or dialect, or `auto` [mm-t2a-http].

**Turn-taking.** Not applicable to a synthesis model.

### Sampling and API parameters

- `voice_setting` (`voice_id`, `speed`, `vol`, `pitch`, `emotion`, `text_normalization`, `latex_read`), `audio_setting`, `pronunciation_dict`, `language_boost`, `voice_modify`, `subtitle_enable`, `stream` and `output_format` [mm-t2a-http].
- The pages read list no temperature or seed.
- The text-to-audio limit is 60 requests a minute per key [mm-limits].

### Migrating from the previous generation

From Speech 2.6 (`speech-2.6-hd` and `speech-2.6-turbo`, 2025-10-29) [mm-t2a-http] [mm-release]:

- Interjection tags arrive on 2.8 and are not available on 2.6.
- The `fluent` and `whisper` emotion values stay on 2.6 only.
- 2.6 has the emotion field but no inline tags. Because of this it has no file here.
- The list prices are unchanged: US$100 per 1M characters for HD and US$60 for Turbo [mm-paygo].

## What the system card reports

MiniMax publishes no system card in the pages read. Its voice-cloning API carries consent and deletion rules in the reference [mm-voice-clone].

## Behaviour observed in practice

- **Rank (M).** Artificial Analysis gives a quality Elo of 1153.28 for Speech 2.8 Turbo, against 1172.76 for 2.8 HD and 1123.66 for 2.6 Turbo [aa-tts-board].
- **Throughput (M).** About 111 characters a second in the same data [aa-tts-board].
- No practitioner report was read.

## Benchmarks

Independent: Artificial Analysis quality Elo 1153.28 [aa-tts-board]. No maker benchmark was found.
