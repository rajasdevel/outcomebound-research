# Scribe v2 Medical

Scribe v2 Medical is a batch transcription model of the Scribe Medical class, generation 2. It is a fine-tune of Scribe v2 for clinical audio. ElevenLabs says it recognises drug names, anatomy, pathology and clinical dictation better, and matches Scribe v2 on everyday speech. The changelog entry of 2026-09-11 announced general availability [el-models] [el-changelog-scribe-med]. Family-wide rules are in the [ElevenLabs README](README.md). The request fields are those of [Scribe v2](scribe_v2.md).

## How to instruct it

### Setting up the prompt

The `model_id` is `scribe_v2_medical` on `POST /v1/speech-to-text`. The request shape is the same as for Scribe v2: `keyterms`, `entity_detection`, `no_verbatim`, diarisation and timestamps. The models page lists the languages, features and price as the same too [el-models] [el-changelog-scribe-med].

- **Keyterms.** The keyterm guide lists Scribe v2 Medical (batch) among the models that take keyterms, up to 1,000 [el-stt-keyterm].
- **Entities.** Entity detection includes protected health information (PHI) categories. The models page pairs it with compliance workflows [el-models] [el-api-stt].
- **Intended purpose.** The models page states that the model makes draft transcripts of clinical audio for documentation and administrative work, for review and correction by a health professional. It states that the model does not interpret clinical information or give diagnoses or treatment advice [el-models].

### Reasoning and effort

None. ElevenLabs describes no reasoning control for Scribe v2 Medical.

### Output: format, length, tone

The output is a transcript as for Scribe v2. The limits are those of Scribe v2: up to 10 hours of audio in standard mode [el-models] [el-stt-overview].

### Tools and agents

Not applicable. It is a batch model. For live clinical audio the models page points to Scribe v2 Realtime [el-models].

### Coding

As for Scribe v2 [el-api-stt].

### Long context and retrieval

As for Scribe v2: parallel transcription of files over 8 minutes [el-stt-overview].

### Structured output

As for Scribe v2 [el-api-stt].

### Images, audio, other inputs

Audio and video files. The models page names clinician-patient conversations, dictation, intake calls and care-coordination calls as sources [el-models].

### Sampling and API parameters

As for Scribe v2. The model is HIPAA-eligible. Business Associate Agreements are for Enterprise customers. Zero Retention Mode (`enable_logging=false`) deletes the audio and text of a request when it ends. ElevenLabs retains nothing, and the application keeps the transcripts under its own controls [el-models] [el-zrm].

### Migrating from the previous generation

There is no earlier medical model. To move from Scribe v2, change `model_id`. The price stays at US$0.22 per hour [el-models] [el-pricing-api].

## What the system card reports

ElevenLabs publishes no system card. The intended-purpose note above is the closest document, and the models page says that companies needing HIPAA compliance must sign a Business Associate Agreement before sending protected health information [el-models].

## Behaviour observed in practice

No independent measurement was found. Artificial Analysis lists no entry for Scribe v2 Medical (read 2026-10-03) [aa-stt-board]. The maker claims 35% fewer transcription errors on clinical audio than Scribe v2, and no loss on everyday speech. The pages read give no dataset or method [el-models].

## Benchmarks

None independent. Maker claim: 35% fewer errors than Scribe v2 on clinical audio [el-models].

## Open questions

- The dataset and method behind the 35% claim.
- Independent error rates on clinical audio, drug names and accented speech.
- Whether realtime clinical transcription is planned. The models page calls this model batch only.
