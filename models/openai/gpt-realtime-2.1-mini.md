# GPT-Realtime-2.1 Mini

GPT-Realtime-2.1 Mini (`gpt-realtime-2.1-mini`) is OpenAI's smaller, cheaper speech-to-speech reasoning model in the Realtime API. OpenAI released it on 2026-07-06 with GPT-Realtime-2.1. It is generation 2.1 of the GPT Realtime Mini class, and it replaced GPT-Realtime Mini. OpenAI describes it as a distilled reasoning model that is quicker and costs less for live voice use, and its audio rates are about one third of GPT-Realtime-2.1's [openai-changelog] [openai-model-gpt-realtime-2.1-mini] [openai-pricing]. OpenAI also names it as the replacement for its old text-to-speech models [openai-deprecations]. The [OpenAI README](README.md) holds the Realtime API guide.

## How to instruct it

### Setting up the prompt

- OpenAI has no guide for the Mini model alone. Its prompting guide for Realtime models names GPT-Realtime-2 and 1.5 only, and its cost guide says to test with the larger model first, refine the prompt, and then try the mini model, because the mini model is usually weaker at instruction following and function calling [openai-guide-voice-prompting] [openai-guide-voice-cost].
- OpenAI's December 2025 note on the earlier Mini snapshot says the gap between mini and full-size models had narrowed for tool calling and instruction following. No source gives the same measure for 2.1 Mini [openai-blog-audio-updates].
- The prompt structure, the literal reading of instructions and the advice to keep rules few apply as in the [GPT-Realtime-2 file](gpt-realtime-2.md).

### Reasoning and effort

- **Levels.** `minimal`, `low`, `medium`, `high`, `xhigh`. The default is `low` according to an OpenAI staff reply, which refers to Realtime models that support reasoning. A developer's copy of OpenAI's model list also shows `low` for this model [community-default-reasoning].
- **What is measured.** Artificial Analysis: Big Bench Audio 62.7% at Minimal and 74.7% at High; tau-Voice 22.5% and 29.4%. Time to first audio is 0.85 s at Minimal and 4.28 s at High, about five times longer for 12 points more on Big Bench Audio [aa-speech-to-speech].
- **Use.** OpenAI gives no advice on effort for the Mini model. In the Artificial Analysis numbers, High effort removes most of the model's speed advantage (inference from the figures above).

### Output: format, length, tone

- No Mini-specific advice. The guide's length, preamble and channel advice for GPT-Realtime-2 is the only published guidance.

### Tools and agents

- Function calling and MCP follow the Realtime API. The model page lists function calling and prompt caching as the features [openai-model-gpt-realtime-2.1-mini].
- Artificial Analysis measured tau-Voice task completion at 29.4% (High) and 22.5% (Minimal), against 45.7% and 38.0% for GPT-Realtime-2.1 [aa-speech-to-speech]. OpenAI's cost guide says the mini size is weaker at function calling and to test the larger model first [openai-guide-voice-cost].

### Coding

OpenAI gives no coding guidance for this model.

### Long context and retrieval

- 128,000-token window and 32,000-token maximum output, as for the full model [openai-model-gpt-realtime-2.1-mini].
- Cached audio input costs $0.30 per million tokens against $10 uncached; cached text costs $0.06 against $0.60 [openai-pricing].

### Structured output

No structured-output or JSON-mode feature is listed. The pages read describe no schema mechanism other than a function's JSON-schema parameters [openai-model-gpt-realtime-2.1-mini].

### Images, audio, other inputs

- Text, audio and image in; text and audio out. The model page says the model takes audio and text over WebRTC, WebSocket or SIP, and also lists image input; image rates are $0.80 in and $0.08 cached [openai-model-gpt-realtime-2.1-mini] [openai-pricing].
- Audio formats are those of the Realtime API: PCM 16-bit mono at 24 kHz, G.711 mu-law, G.711 A-law [openai-ref-client-secrets].

### Voice: turn-taking, interruption and speech style

- **Turn detection and interruption.** The same as for the other Realtime models: `server_vad`, `semantic_vad`, `create_response`, `interrupt_response`, noise reduction, push-to-talk with `null`; interruption cancels the response, and WebRTC and SIP truncate unplayed audio on the server [openai-guide-vad] [openai-guide-conversations].
- **Latency.** Artificial Analysis: 0.85 s to first audio at Minimal effort, against 1.12 s for GPT-Realtime-2 and 0.97 s for GPT-Realtime-2.1 at the same setting, and 4.28 s at High. The Conversational Dynamics score is about 91.7% to 91.8% at both settings, against 92.7% to 95.7% for GPT-Realtime-2.1 [aa-speech-to-speech].
- **Voices.** The ten built-in voices, with marin and cedar recommended, and custom voices for approved customers [openai-guide-conversations].
- **Prompting for speech.** No Mini-specific advice. OpenAI advises testing the full model first. No source tests preambles, silence handling or exact-entity capture on the Mini model.

### Sampling and API parameters

- No `temperature` in the GA interface. Sessions last at most 60 minutes [openai-blog-realtime-api] [openai-guide-conversations].
- Rate limits: tier 1, 200 requests and 40,000 tokens a minute; tier 5, 20,000 requests and 15 million tokens [openai-model-gpt-realtime-2.1-mini].

### Migrating from the previous generation

From GPT-Realtime Mini, which shuts down on 2027-01-20, OpenAI names this model as the replacement. The older snapshot `gpt-realtime-mini-2025-10-06` shut down on 2026-07-23. Text and audio rates are the same as for the older model ($0.60 and $2.40 for text, $10 and $20 for audio). The window grows from 32,000 to 128,000 tokens, maximum output from 4,096 to 32,000, and reasoning effort is new [openai-deprecations] [openai-model-gpt-realtime-mini] [openai-pricing].

Artificial Analysis lists the older Mini (an entry it labels October 2025) at 56.8 on its Speech to Speech Index, above 2.1 Mini at Minimal effort (52.8), although 2.1 Mini scores higher on tau-Voice (22.5% against 15.1%) [aa-speech-to-speech].

## What the system card reports

OpenAI lists no system card or model card for GPT-Realtime-2.1 Mini on 2026-10-03 [openai-hub]. The GPT-Live card covers GPT-Live-1 and GPT-Live-1 mini and not this model.

## Behaviour observed in practice

- **Independent measurement (M).** Artificial Analysis lists the model at Minimal (index 52.8; rank 25 in its Arena) and at High (no index). Its Arena preference Elo at Minimal is 839.89 [aa-speech-to-speech].
- **Cloud (L).** Microsoft Foundry lists it, dated 2026-07-07, as a smaller variant of 2.1 [ms-foundry-models].
- **Practitioner reports (A).** In OpenAI's launch thread, one developer reported on 2026-07-09 that 2.1 Mini followed prompt rules more consistently than GPT-Realtime Mini and recognised Portuguese names better, but did not call a function tool in a SIP flow where the older Mini did, with the same prompt, tools and accept payload. Another developer reported on 2026-08-03, after moving a structured outbound telephone agent from GPT-Realtime Mini, that 2.1 Mini narrated its own steps, put invented transition text in the commentary phase, matched inline examples in transition rules literally, and needed more prompt work; the older Mini behaved more predictably for that flow [community-realtime-21].
