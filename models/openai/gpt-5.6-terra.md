# GPT-5.6 Terra

GPT-5.6 Terra (`gpt-5.6-terra`) is the middle model of OpenAI's GPT-5.6 family, released on 2026-07-09 with
Sol and Luna. OpenAI describes it as strong performance at a lower price and as comparable to the mini-class
models of earlier GPT-5 generations; it is the replacement OpenAI names for the GPT-5 mini snapshot shutting
down on 2026-12-11. Its price, $2 in and $12 out per million tokens, followed a 20% cut on 2026-07-30. There is
no GPT-6 Terra, and OpenAI's GPT-6 guide offers Astra, Sol and Luna only, so GPT-5.6 Terra is the latest
model of its class. The family guidance (prompting guides, effort migration, programmatic tool calling and the GPT-5.6 system-card findings) is in the
[OpenAI README](README.md); this file records what differs for Terra.

## How to instruct it

### Setting up the prompt

- OpenAI's GPT-5.6 prompting guide is written for Sol and applies to the family: outcome-first prompts, one
  autonomy policy, one statement per rule, no unrelated tools, and a suggested layout of role, goal, success
  criteria, constraints, tools, output and stop rules [openai-gpt56-prompting]. OpenAI publishes no
  Terra-specific variant.
- The guide says GPT-5.6 models follow prompt contracts closely, so contradictions cause more trouble than
  missing detail; that applies to Terra as to Sol [openai-gpt56-prompting].
- Terra is a smaller model than Sol. OpenAI's card says its larger models outperform Terra and Luna on complex
  tasks while avoiding edit conflicts with user changes [openai-56-card].

### Reasoning and effort

- **Levels.** `none`, `low`, `medium` (default), `high`, `xhigh`, `max` [openai-model-gpt-5.6-terra].
- **OpenAI's rule.** Migrating from GPT-5.5 or 5.4, keep the effort, then test one level lower; compare `max`
  with `xhigh` [openai-guide-gpt-5.6].
- **Measured scaling.** Artificial Analysis's Intelligence Index: 27.5 (low), 30.1 (medium), 34.2 (high), 38.0
  (xhigh), 42.1 (max). Its Terminal-Bench 4.0 run: 1.5, 1.0, 1.5, 10.1, 35.4, near zero below `xhigh`. Cost per Index task: $0.14 at low, $0.34 high, $0.63 xhigh, $1.40
  max [aa-model-gpt-5-6-terra].
- **Persisted reasoning and pro mode.** As for the family: `reasoning.context` defaults to `all_turns`; the
  `reasoning.mode: pro` option works with any GPT-5.6 model; reasoning is shared within the GPT-5.6 family,
  so a conversation can move between Sol, Terra and Luna and keep it [openai-reasoning-guide].
- **What not to do.** OpenAI says effort is a tuning knob and not the main way to recover quality
  [openai-reasoning-guide].

### Output: format, length, tone

- GPT-5.6 is more concise by default than GPT-5.5; OpenAI recommends `text.verbosity` for the default level
  of detail and naming what a short answer must contain [openai-gpt56-prompting].
- Artificial Analysis counted 120 million output tokens for its Intelligence Index run, against 90 million
  for Sol, so Terra is more verbose than Sol on that run [aa-model-gpt-5-6-terra].

### Tools and agents

- Tools, programmatic tool calling and the multi-agent beta are available to all GPT-5.6 models; the
  routing and programmatic-calling advice is in the [README](README.md#prompting-guides) [openai-guide-gpt-5.6]
  [openai-gpt56-prompting].
- In OpenAI's prompt-injection tests Terra scored 1.000 in connector scenarios and 0.946 in search and
  function-calling scenarios, higher than Sol (0.910) and Luna (0.897) in the latter [openai-56-card].
- Artificial Analysis lists no Coding Agent Index row for Terra [aa-coding-agents].

### Coding

- Scale's SWE-Bench Pro V2 lists Terra (Codex, xhigh) at 86.3, above GPT-5.6 Sol's 82.4 in the same table;
  neither row is grayed, which Scale's legend reads as uncapped cost and a 250-turn limit for both. The tbench.ai leaderboard has Terra at
  21.5% on Terminal-Bench 4.0 (Codex, max), against 37.3% for Sol; Artificial Analysis's own run gives 35.4%
  [scale-swe-pro-v2] [tbench-4-0] [aa-model-gpt-5-6-terra].
- OpenAI's validation and frontend advice for the family (also in the [README](README.md#prompting-guides)): give the model tools that validate its output and say which validation matters, such as targeted tests, type or lint checks, build checks, a minimal smoke test, and an explanation when validation cannot run; for frontend work keep existing design tokens and components, add no unrequested features, keep responsive behaviour, and render and inspect the result before finishing [openai-gpt56-prompting].
- The card rates Terra High for cyber, with a safeguards posture tailored to it: activation classifiers
  are used for Sol and Terra [openai-56-card].

### Long context and retrieval

- 1,050,000-token window, at most 922,000 input tokens, 128,000 output; above 272,000 input tokens the whole
  request bills at twice the input and cache rates and 1.5 times the output rate
  [openai-model-gpt-5.6-terra].
- Artificial Analysis's AA-LCR: 83.0% at max [aa-model-gpt-5-6-terra]. Caching follows the GPT-5.6 rules
  (explicit mode, 1,024-token minimum, 30-minute lifetime, write at 1.25 times input)
  [openai-prompt-caching].

### Structured output

- Strict structured outputs and function schemas are supported; see the README [openai-structured-outputs]
  [openai-function-calling].

### Images, audio, other inputs

- Text and image in, text out, with the GPT-5.6 sizing rules (`low` 512 by 512 pixels; `high` 2,048 by 2,048 and
  2,500 patches; `original` and `auto` keep dimensions up to 65,535 pixels a side; images over 30,000 patches
  are rejected) [openai-images-vision]. Artificial Analysis's MMMU-Pro gives 80.7% at max.

### Sampling and API parameters

- No sampling-parameter rule for GPT-5.6 appears on the GPT-5.6 pages read; OpenAI's deployment checklist
  states the removal of `temperature` and `top_p` above effort `none` in its GPT-6 migration paragraph
  [openai-deployment-checklist]. Fast mode costs twice the standard rate ($4
  in, $24 out); Batch and Flex are half price; tier 5 limits are 15,000 requests and 40 million tokens a
  minute [openai-model-gpt-5.6-terra] [openai-pricing].
- OpenAI asks applications serving individual end users to send a stable `safety_identifier`, since
  real-time classifiers can block or pause requests [openai-guide-gpt-5.6].

### Migrating from the previous generation

- From GPT-5 mini or GPT-5.4 mini, OpenAI's pages name Terra as the replacement for GPT-5 mini and compare its
  price with GPT-5.4 Mini ($0.75 in, $4.50 out) [openai-deprecations] [openai-model-gpt-5.6-terra].
- The migration steps (effort baseline, Responses API, `reasoning.context`, cache parameters, trimmed prompts)
  are in the [README](README.md#migrating-to-gpt-56-from-55-or-54).

## What the system card reports

Source: the GPT-5.6 system card, covering Sol, Terra and Luna [openai-56-card]. Numbers are OpenAI's; the family-wide
list is in the [README](README.md#gpt-56-system-card-findings).

- **Preparedness.** High in biology and cyber, below High in AI self-improvement; the cyber rule-out for critical
  exploits was run on Sol and applied to Terra and Luna as smaller models.
- **Biology.** Tacit-knowledge test 84.1% when refusals are accounted for, the highest of the new models and
  above the 80% expert-consensus threshold, though below GPT-5.5, which OpenAI attributes to saturation or
  noise.
- **Safeguards.** Activation classifiers (Sol and Terra), a two-tier classifier system and a safety reasoner;
  a safeguards posture tailored to each model's capability.
- **Capabilities.** HealthBench Professional 57.7; in health tests OpenAI says Terra and Luna retain much of
  Sol's performance despite their lower cost; controllability of Terra's chain of thought was low, like
  earlier models.
- **Prompt injection.** Connector scenarios 1.000; search and function-calling scenarios 0.946.

## Behaviour observed in practice

- **Independent measurement (M).** Artificial Analysis: Intelligence Index 42 at max ($1.40 a task, 102 tokens a
  second), hallucination rate 87.9% on AA-Omniscience, IFBench 71.2% [aa-model-gpt-5-6-terra]. Unlike Sol
  and Luna, its model page does not mark Terra deprecated.
- **Launch price (A).** A secondary launch report gives $2.50 in and $15 out, which matches the 20% cut of
  2026-07-30 [rits-gpt-5-6-launch].
- **Cloud (L).** Amazon Bedrock lists it with a launch date of 2026-07-13; Microsoft Foundry lists
  `gpt-5.6-terra` dated 2026-07-09 [aws-card-gpt-5-6-terra] [ms-foundry-models].
- **Practitioner reports (A).** None first-hand were gathered.
