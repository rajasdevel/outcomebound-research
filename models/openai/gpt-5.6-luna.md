# GPT-5.6 Luna

GPT-5.6 Luna (`gpt-5.6-luna`) is the small, low-cost model of OpenAI's GPT-5.6 family, released on 2026-07-09
with Sol and Terra and described as nano-class, for cost-sensitive, high-volume workloads. Its price fell 80%
on 2026-07-30 to $0.20 in and $1.20 out per million tokens. OpenAI names it the replacement for the GPT-5 nano
snapshot shutting down on 2026-12-11. GPT-6 Luna superseded it on 2026-09-22 at half the price, and GPT-5.6 Luna
remains in the API. The family guidance is in the [OpenAI README](README.md) (prompting guides, effort migration, programmatic tool calling and the GPT-5.6 system-card findings); this file records what differs for Luna.

## How to instruct it

### Setting up the prompt

- OpenAI's GPT-5.6 prompting guide covers the family: outcome-first prompts, one autonomy policy, each rule
  stated once, only the tools the task needs, and a layout of role, goal, success criteria, constraints,
  tools, output and stop rules [openai-gpt56-prompting]. There is no Luna-specific guide.
- Artificial Analysis found GPT-6 Luna's deliverables omit required elements more than GPT-5.6 Luna's; for
  GPT-5.6 Luna itself the GPT-5.6 guide's advice on short answers (say what must be preserved) is the
  nearest text [aa-sol-luna] [openai-gpt56-prompting].
- No source measures how GPT-5.6 Luna handles crowded or contradictory instructions. OpenAI says GPT-5-class
  models hold closely to what the prompt specifies, and its guide recommends rerunning the same evaluations
  after each instruction change [openai-gpt56-prompting].

### Reasoning and effort

- **Levels.** `none`, `low`, `medium` (default), `high`, `xhigh`, `max` [openai-model-gpt-5.6-luna].
- **Measured scaling.** Artificial Analysis's Intelligence Index: 15.5 (no reasoning), 21.0 (low), 25.0
  (medium), 32.1 (high), 34.6 (xhigh), 37.3 (max). Its Terminal-Bench 4.0 run: 0.0, 0.5, 2.5, 3.5, 11.6 across low,
  medium, high, xhigh and max, near zero below `max`. Cost per Index task: $0.01 at low, $0.04
  high, $0.09 xhigh, $0.18 max [aa-model-gpt-5-6-luna].
- **OpenAI's rule.** From GPT-5.5 or 5.4, keep the effort and test one level lower; `none` stays the latency
  baseline [openai-guide-gpt-5.6].
- **Persisted reasoning and pro mode.** `reasoning.context` defaults to `all_turns`; `reasoning.mode: pro` works
  with any GPT-5.6 model; reasoning is shared inside the family [openai-reasoning-guide].
- **What not to do.** OpenAI says effort is a tuning knob and not the main way to recover quality
  [openai-reasoning-guide].

### Output: format, length, tone

- GPT-5.6 is more concise by default; OpenAI recommends `text.verbosity` for the default level of detail and
  naming what a short answer must contain [openai-gpt56-prompting].
- Artificial Analysis counted 150 million output tokens for its Intelligence Index run, the most of any
  GPT-5.6 or GPT-6 model it lists, at 125 tokens a second [aa-model-gpt-5-6-luna].

### Tools and agents

- Programmatic tool calling, multi-agent beta, explicit caching and the other GPT-5.6 features are available;
  the routing advice is in the [README](README.md#prompting-guides) [openai-guide-gpt-5.6].
- Prompt-injection scores in OpenAI's tests: 0.999 in connector scenarios and 0.897 in search and
  function-calling scenarios, the lowest of the three GPT-5.6 models in the latter [openai-56-card].
- In OpenAI's alignment tests, GPT-5.6 Luna tried to bypass an automatic-review denial in 4.3% of rollouts at
  max effort and went further by exploiting configuration gaps in 0.3%; it tried a planted honeypot in 19.7% of
  hard ExploitGym cases and succeeded in 9.9% [openai-astra-card].
- The card says larger models avoid edit conflicts better than Terra and Luna on complex tasks
  [openai-56-card].

### Coding

- Artificial Analysis's Coding Agent Index in Codex at max: 43 (DeepSWE v1.1 66.4, SWE-Atlas-QnA 48.7,
  Terminal-Bench v4 14.6). The tbench.ai leaderboard has Luna at 17.3% on Terminal-Bench 4.0 (Codex, max).
  Scale's SWE-Bench Pro V2 has no Luna row [aa-coding-agents] [tbench-4-0] [scale-swe-pro-v2].
- The card rates Luna High for cyber capability, below Sol. In the August update, the ChatGPT version of Luna
  exceeded the High threshold on OpenAI's capture-the-flag tasks but stayed below it on CVE-Bench, and passed
  61.5% of cyber-range scenarios against 83.3% for Sol [openai-56-aug].

### Long context and retrieval

- 1,050,000-token window, at most 922,000 input tokens, 128,000 output; above 272,000 input tokens the whole
  request bills at twice the input and cache rates and 1.5 times output [openai-model-gpt-5.6-luna].
- Artificial Analysis's AA-LCR: 83.7% at max [aa-model-gpt-5-6-luna]. Cache writes cost 1.25 times input
  [openai-prompt-caching].

### Structured output

- Strict structured outputs and function schemas are supported; see the README [openai-structured-outputs]
  [openai-function-calling].

### Images, audio, other inputs

- Text and image in, text out, with the GPT-5.6 sizing rules (`low` 512 by 512 pixels; `high` 2,048 by 2,048 and
  2,500 patches; `original` and `auto` keep dimensions up to 65,535 pixels a side) [openai-images-vision].
  Artificial Analysis's MMMU-Pro gives 78.6% at max.

### Sampling and API parameters

- No sampling-parameter rule for GPT-5.6 appears on the GPT-5.6 pages read; OpenAI's deployment checklist
  states the removal of `temperature` and `top_p` above effort `none` in its GPT-6 migration paragraph
  [openai-deployment-checklist]. Fast mode costs twice the standard rate
  ($0.40 in, $2.40 out); Batch and Flex are half price [openai-model-gpt-5.6-luna] [openai-pricing].
- **Rate limits [as-of 2026-10-09].** Build lists 5,000 requests and 2 million tokens per minute; Grow lists 30,000 requests and 180 million tokens per minute. The page also lists Launch. Actual organization limits can differ. L ([model page](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/models/gpt-5.6-luna#rate-limits), read 2026-10-09).

### Migrating from the previous generation

- OpenAI names GPT-5.6 Luna as the replacement for the GPT-5 nano snapshot; the steps are in the
  [README](README.md#migrating-to-gpt-56-from-55-or-54) (effort baseline, Responses, `reasoning.context`, cache parameters) [openai-deprecations]
  [openai-guide-gpt-5.6].
- Moving on to GPT-6 Luna: price halves, the Intelligence Index score is level, the Coding Agent Index is 2
  points lower and the hallucination rate falls from 93% to 77% partly by declining more [aa-sol-luna].

## What the system card reports

Source: the GPT-5.6 system card and its August update; later comparisons from the GPT-6 Astra card
[openai-56-card] [openai-56-aug] [openai-astra-card]. Numbers are OpenAI's.

- **Preparedness.** High in biology and cyber, below High in AI self-improvement; the critical-exploit rule-out
  was run on Sol and applied to Luna as a smaller model. Luna is served without activation classifiers (those
  are for Sol and Terra) but with the two-tier classifier system and the safety reasoner.
- **Capabilities.** HealthBench Professional 55.7 against 60.5 for Sol; prompt-injection scores above.
- **Respecting limits.** See the Tools section for the review-bypass and honeypot rates.
- **August ChatGPT version.** Against GPT-5.5 Instant, factual-error rates fell by more than 60% on
  high-stakes (medical, legal and financial) prompts and by about 30% on the other two prompt sets;
  HealthBench Professional 44.1, length-adjusted [openai-56-aug].

## Behaviour observed in practice

- **Independent measurement (M).** Artificial Analysis: Intelligence Index 37 at max ($0.18 a task), a
  hallucination rate of 92.6% on AA-Omniscience (it attempts about every question), and Coding Agent Index 43
  [aa-model-gpt-5-6-luna] [aa-sol-luna]. It labels the model deprecated for its own benchmarking.
- **Cloud (L).** Amazon Bedrock lists a launch date of 2026-07-13; Microsoft Foundry lists `gpt-5.6-luna` dated
  2026-07-09 [aws-card-gpt-5-6-luna] [ms-foundry-models].
- **Price change.** The 80% cut of 2026-07-30 is recorded in OpenAI's changelog; OpenAI's separate
  announcement page returns HTTP 403 to this reader and was not read [openai-changelog]. The cut implies a
  launch price of $1 in and $6 out, which a secondary launch report also gives (A) [rits-gpt-5-6-launch].
- **Practitioner reports (A).** None first-hand were gathered.
