# Qwen-Audio-3.1-TTS-Flash

Qwen-Audio-3.1-TTS-Flash is the Flash tier of generation 3.1 of Alibaba's hosted Qwen-Audio speech synthesis line. It turns text into speech, and its delivery can be directed with a free-text instruction and with inline emotion and nonverbal tags, so it is a voice actor model. QwenCloud's changelog dates it 2026-10-12. Alibaba names it for low-latency use: voice assistants, live conversation and customer service. The generation before it, `qwen-audio-3.0-tts-flash`, is still sold and has no file. [Qwen-Audio-3.1-TTS-Plus](qwen-audio-3.1-tts-plus.md) is the quality tier of the same generation. Family-wide rules are in the [Alibaba README](README.md) [qwencloud-changelog] [qwencloud-page-tts31flash] [qwencloud-tts-models].

## How to instruct it

### Setting up the prompt

There is no system prompt. A synthesis task has three parts that shape the result [qwencloud-tts-guide] [qwencloud-tts-events]:

- `text`: the words to speak, with optional inline tags (see the voice section).
- `instruction`: one free-text instruction for the whole task. It sets the language, a dialect, the emotion, the pace or the speaking style.
- `voice`: a system voice, a cloned voice or a designed voice.

Alibaba recommends that `instruction` names the synthesis language and the effect you want. The language of the instruction must match the language of the text. The guide's example asks, in Chinese, for Dutch speech with every word said slowly [qwencloud-tts-guide].

### Reasoning and effort

None. The model has no reasoning control.

### Output: format, length, tone

`format` is `pcm`, `wav`, `mp3` (default) or `opus`. `sample_rate` is 8000, 16000, 22050 (default), 24000, 44100 or 48000 Hz. `bit_rate` (6 to 510 kbps) applies to mp3 and opus. `volume` runs from 0 to 100 (default 50), and `rate` and `pitch` from 0.5 to 2.0 (default 1.0). `seed` (0 to 65535) gives the same result for the same model version, text, voice and settings. `word_timestamp_enabled` returns word timings in streaming mode, for cloned voices and some system voices [qwencloud-tts-events].

`enable_aigc_tag` embeds an AIGC watermark in wav, mp3 or opus output. It is off by default, and two fields set the propagator and the propagation id [qwencloud-tts-events].

### Tools and agents

Not applicable. The model synthesises speech only. For a conversation model, see the [Qwen-Audio realtime files](qwen-audio-3.1-realtime-flash.md).

### Coding

Alibaba gives no coding guidance for this model.

### Long context and retrieval

A WebSocket task takes text in `continue-task` messages of up to 20,000 characters each and 200,000 characters in total. The gap between messages must stay under 23 seconds, or the connection times out. One connection can run several tasks in a row: each needs a new `task_id`, and an idle connection closes 60 seconds after a task ends [qwencloud-tts-events] [qwencloud-tts-guide].

### Structured output

Not applicable.

### Images, audio, other inputs

Text is the input. A voice clone takes a reference recording: WAV (16-bit), MP3 or M4A, 10 to 20 seconds advised and at most 60 seconds, at most 10 MB, at 16 kHz or more, with at least 5 seconds of continuous clear reading and no music, noise or other speakers. A cloned or designed voice works only with the target model named when it was made [qwencloud-voice-cloning] [qwencloud-tts-guide].

### Voice: turn-taking, interruption and speech style

Turn-taking and interruption do not apply: the model does not listen. A running task can be cancelled with `finish-task` and `directive` set to `cancel` [qwencloud-tts-guide] [qwencloud-tts-events].

**Direct the whole task with `instruction`.** System, cloned and designed voices of the 3.1 and 3.0 Qwen-Audio TTS models all take a natural-language instruction. Alibaba's examples are a gentle and slower delivery, an excited broadcast style, and a dialect ("speak Shanghainese"). For a cloned voice, a dialect is set only by instruction. For a system voice, pick one that speaks the dialect, or one that takes a dialect instruction [qwencloud-tts-models] [qwencloud-tts-guide].

**Direct part of the text with tags.** Tags go in square brackets in `text` and need no extra parameter. A control tag sets the emotion or style of the text after it until the next control tag; when the service splits a long sentence, the effect ends at the end of the split segment. A nonverbal tag inserts a sound where it stands and does not change the style around it. Alibaba lists two sets for the 3.1 and 3.0 models [qwencloud-tts-guide]:

- 23 control tags: `[sad]`, `[amazed]`, `[deep and loud shouting]`, `[trembling]`, `[angry]`, `[excited]`, `[sarcastic]`, `[curious]`, `[like dracula]`, `[bored]`, `[tired]`, `[scornful]`, `[shouting]`, `[asmr]`, `[panicked]`, `[mischievously]`, `[empathetic]`, `[whispers]`, `[reluctantly]`, `[crying]`, `[serious]`, `[very slowly]` and `[very fast]`.
- 7 nonverbal tags: `[gasp]`, `[sighing]`, `[clears throat]`, `[giggles]`, `[laughing]`, `[cough]` and `[snorts]`.

The guide's examples start a line with `[excited]` and insert `[laughing]` between two sentences, or switch from `[serious]` to `[excited]` at a sentence break [qwencloud-tts-guide].

**Pronunciation and language.** `language_hints` sets the target language and guides how numbers, abbreviations and symbols are read; only the first element of the array is used. `hot_fix` assigns pinyin to a word or replaces a word before synthesis. SSML (`enable_ssml`) controls pauses, reading rules and pronunciation; with SSML on, the whole text must go in one message [qwencloud-tts-guide] [qwencloud-tts-events].

**Voices.** The voice list for this model holds 139 voice IDs (counted; the page states no total). Singapore and China North 2 (Beijing) serve 4 multilingual and dialect voices, 15 premium English voices and 51 additional-language voices, one for each extra language. Only China North 2 (Beijing) serves the 42 premium Chinese voices and 27 additional system voices. Voice design (a voice from a written description) and voice cloning are both supported on 3.1. 3.0 had cloning only, with no voice design [qwencloud-tts-voices] [qwencloud-tts-models].

### Sampling and API parameters

`seed` is the only sampling-like control. The rest are the audio settings above. The model page gives a rate limit of 180 requests per minute [qwencloud-tts-events] [qwencloud-page-tts31flash].

### Migrating from the previous generation

From `qwen-audio-3.0-tts-flash`: change the model name and pick a 3.1 voice. A cloned voice must be made again for the new target model. The tag sets and the `instruction` field are the same on both generations. 3.1 adds voice design, HTTP access as well as WebSocket, and many more languages. The billing unit also changes: 3.0 Flash costs US$0.15 per 10,000 characters, while 3.1 Flash bills input and output tokens [qwencloud-tts-models] [qwencloud-tts-guide] [qwencloud-prices]. Alibaba's notes say 3.1 is more robust to noise and reverberation in a cloning clip [qwencloud-changelog].
