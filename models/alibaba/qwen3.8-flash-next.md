# Qwen3.8-Flash-Next

Qwen3.8-Flash-Next is the open-weight release of Alibaba's Flash-size 3.8 model, in the Qwen Flash class at generation
3.8. It is a mixture-of-experts vision-language model with 125 billion parameters, 6 billion active per token, and a
further 51 billion parameters of n-gram embeddings held off the accelerator. Alibaba presents it as an experimental
preview of the architecture planned for Qwen 4, built for cheap long-context agent, coding and office work, and
points production users to the hosted [Qwen3.8-Flash](qwen3.8-flash.md) [hf-flash-next] [blog-flash-next]. Its
size sits well below the 397B-A17B Qwen3.7-Plus that its card compares against. Family-wide rules are in the
[Alibaba README](README.md).

## How to instruct it

### Setting up the prompt

Alibaba gives no model-specific guidance on system prompts, roles, structure, examples or document placement for
Flash-Next; the card's "Best Practices" covers sampling, output length, YaRN and video settings [hf-flash-next], and
QwenCloud's general prompt-design page is summarised in the README [qwencloud-prompting]. The shipped chat template
is identical to the 27B's: system message first, no images or video in the system message, XML-like tool calls, and
an effort sentence in the system turn for `xhigh` and `low` only [hf-flash-next-template].

### Reasoning and effort

Thinking is on by default and is a hybrid: `enable_thinking` can turn it off, `reasoning_effort` takes `xhigh`
(default), `medium` or `low`, and `preserve_thinking` (on by default) keeps thinking from every earlier message
[hf-flash-next]. The card warns that in multi-turn agent work a lower effort does not always reduce total
completion time, because per-turn speed gains can bring more failures, retries and tokens [hf-flash-next].
Where an engine takes separate limits, the card suggests 262,144 reasoning tokens and 131,072 reply tokens. On the
independent index the model used 240M output tokens against a median of 140M, effort not stated
[aa-qwen3-8-flash-next]. No effort-level measurement is published.

### Output: format, length, tone

No formatting or verbosity guidance. Presence penalty between 0 and 2 can curb endless repetition with a risk of
language mixing [hf-flash-next]. The 131,072 figure is a recommended reply length, not a stated API limit.

### Tools and agents

Alibaba recommends the model for high-volume applications, tool-driven workflows and coding and office assistants,
and lists Claude Code (Anthropic protocol), Codex, Qoder CLI, Qwen Code, OpenClaw and QwenWork as supported harnesses
[blog-flash-next]. The maker's table gives Toolathlon Verified 73.5 (against 67.1 for the 27B and 50.6 for
Qwen3.7-Plus), ClawEval-MM 64.4 pass@3, AndroidWorld 84.5 and OSWorld 2.0 19.4 binary / 52.3 partial
[hf-flash-next]. SGLang's Flash-Next recipe starts the server with the `qwen3` (or `auto`) reasoning parser and the
`auto` tool-call parser; the card also links vLLM and TokenSpeed recipes [sglang-flash-next] [hf-flash-next].
No subagent or context-management guidance is published.

### Coding

Maker-run: SWE-bench Pro 62.5 on a refined set (Claude Code, temperature 1.0, top_p 0.95, 256K context, not the Scale
V2 set), DeepSWE 1.1 58.7 (the higher of two harnesses; best on mini-SWE-agent), SWE-bench Multilingual 81.0
(mini-SWE-agent), NL2Repo-Bench 48.1 (Claude Code, with repository-fetching shell commands disabled), LiveCodeBench
v6 91.9 [hf-flash-next]. These come from the card and no independent coding result exists.

### Long context and retrieval

Native 262,144 tokens, extensible to 1,000,000 with YaRN (factor 4.0; engine flags are on the card). Static
YaRN may hurt short inputs; set it only when needed [hf-flash-next]. The attention is Qwen Sparse Attention over micro-blocks:
Alibaba's technical report says that at 1M tokens it is 7.6 times faster for prefill and 4.9 times faster for
decode than dense attention at kernel level [tech-report-flash-next]. The report's retrieval table compares Qwen Sparse Attention with full attention and does not say it was run on the
released post-trained model: with Qwen Sparse Attention, RULER is 99.9 up to 128K and 93.0 between 512K and 1M, and
8-needle MRCR is 96.0 at 128K, 93.0 at 256K, 40.5 at 512K and 26.4 at 1M, so multi-needle recall drops sharply past
256K (full attention scored lower at 512K and 1M) [tech-report-flash-next]. The post-trained card reports no long-context score. Artificial Analysis lists a 256k
window [aa-qwen3-8-flash-next].

### Structured output

The card describes no structured-output mode; QwenCloud lists the hosted Flash for JSON Schema (strict) mode, which
is on its own file [qwencloud-structured]. A self-hosted engine's constrained decoding is the usual route and is not covered by Alibaba.

### Images, audio, other inputs

Images and video go in as `image_url` and `video_url` parts; the default sampling is 2 frames per second; setting
`longest_edge` to 469,762,048 allows hour-scale video (about 224k tokens) [hf-flash-next]. Vision results (maker-run):
MathVision 90.6 without and 95.7 with code interpreter, CharXiv RQ 84.6 and 90.6, RealWorldQA 88.5, ERQA 72.3, LVBench
76.6, Vision2Web 64.0 [hf-flash-next]. No audio input is listed on the card.

### Sampling and API parameters

Thinking mode: temperature 1.0, top_p 0.95, top_k 20, min_p 0, presence_penalty 0, repetition_penalty 1. Non-thinking
mode: temperature 0.7, top_p 0.80, top_k 20, min_p 0, presence_penalty 1.5 [hf-flash-next]. These are the open
card's values; the hosted Flash defaults to temperature 0.6 when thinking [qwencloud-latest-flash]. Engines named for
production: SGLang, KTransformers and vLLM, latest versions; throughput varies a lot by engine. An FP8 copy of the
weights is also published [hf-flash-next].

### Migrating from the previous generation

The card compares with Qwen3.8-27B, Qwen3.7-Plus, DeepSeek-V4-Flash-0731 and Claude Opus 4.6 (Max) and has no Flash-to-Flash
comparison. Against Qwen3.7-Plus: DeepSWE 1.1 58.7 against 16.5, SWE-bench Pro 62.5 against 55.8, JobBench 55.7 against 27.6,
IFBench 81.3 against 79.1, GPQA Diamond 91.7 against 90.3, HLE 35.9 against 34.7 [hf-flash-next]. The technical report
says the base model leads the 397B-A17B predecessor on eight of fourteen pre-training benchmarks and trails it by at
most 2.6 points on the other six, at about a third of the active parameters and training tokens and about a ninth
of the training compute [tech-report-flash-next]. Alibaba describes the release as an early preview of the Qwen 4 architecture, shared for community evaluation before
the full Qwen 4 family [blog-flash-next].

## What the system card reports

Alibaba has published no system card for Flash-Next. The technical report covers architecture, kernels, optimiser and
pre-training stability, and has no safety or alignment section [tech-report-flash-next]. The card lists no refusal,
sycophancy, deception, injection or cyber and biology findings, and its licence (Qwen Community License 1.0) adds
attribution and commercial-service conditions rather than usage safeguards [hf-flash-next-license].

## Behaviour observed in practice

- **Verbosity and speed (M).** 240M output tokens against a median of 140M, 53.0 output tokens per second, 2.51 s
  to first token, $0.37 per index task [aa-qwen3-8-flash-next].
- **Terminal tasks (M).** Artificial Analysis's chart data puts it at 25.3% on Terminal-Bench 4.0, above the 2.4T
  checkpoint's 11.1% at the same index score [aa-qwen3-8-flash-next] [aa-qwen3-8-2-4t].
- Independent long-context results: none found.

## Benchmarks

Independent: Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v4.3.2, 40 (effort not stated; 240M tokens), at the same score as the
2.4T open checkpoint [aa-qwen3-8-flash-next] [aa-qwen3-8-2-4t]; Terminal-Bench 4.0 25.3% and Terminal-Bench 2.1 86.1% on
Artificial Analysis's own harness, read from chart data [aa-qwen3-8-flash-next]. Scale SWE-Bench Pro V2 and METR list
no entry [scale-swe-pro-v2] [metr-horizons]. Maker-run: DeepSWE 1.1 58.7, SWE-bench Pro 62.5,
SWE-bench Multilingual 81.0, NL2Repo-Bench 48.1, CoWorkBench 73.9 and JobBench 55.7 (in-house), Agents' Last Exam
24.3 pass / 51.2 score, Toolathlon Verified 73.5, IFBench 81.3, GPQA Diamond 91.7, HLE 35.9 (judged by GPT-4o),
LiveCodeBench v6 91.9 [hf-flash-next].
