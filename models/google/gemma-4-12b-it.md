# Gemma 4 12B (instruction-tuned, unified)

Gemma 4 12B Unified is the middle size of the Gemma 4 generation (class Gemma, generation 4): 11.95 billion parameters, 48 layers, open weights under Apache 2.0, and the only Gemma 4 model built without separate vision and audio encoders. Image patches and 40 ms audio chunks are projected straight into the language model's embedding space. It takes text, image, video and audio and returns text, with a 256K window. Google released it on 2026-06-03, two months after the other four sizes, as a laptop-class model (the launch post says 16 GB of VRAM or unified memory is enough). It sits between E4B and 26B A4B, and the launch post calls it Google's first mid-sized Gemma model with native audio input and says it ships with MTP drafters. Its closest predecessor by size is Gemma 3 12B. Google publishes one prompting and formatting guide for all Gemma 4 sizes; this file states what differs for the 12B and links to the [31B file](gemma-4-31b-it.md), which holds the family-wide rules.

## How to instruct it

Family-wide guidance, with the size table, is in [gemma-4-31b-it.md](gemma-4-31b-it.md). Differences for this model follow.

### Setting up the prompt

- Same system, user and model roles, turn markers and think token as the other sizes [gemma-prompt-formatting-4] (L). Instruction following (thinking mode, maker): IFEval 97.2, IFBench 74.0 (above the 26B A4B's 72.0) [gemma-4-tech-report] (L).
- Google publishes no role-prompt, structure or example advice specific to this size.

### Reasoning and effort

- Same on or off switch and no levels. The thinking page says the 12B, 26B A4B and 31B templates insert an empty thought channel when thinking is off, to suppress stray thought channels, and the family model card says every size except E2B and E4B still writes the thought tags with an empty block, so a parser has to accept it. Google's warning that a real thought channel may still appear with thinking off names only the 26B A4B and the 31B [gemma-thinking, gemma-model-card-4, gemma-prompt-formatting-4] (L).
- The Gemini API page for Gemma names only gemma-4-31b-it and gemma-4-26b-a4b-it, so the thinking_level setting is not documented for the 12B there [gemma-on-gemini-api] (L).

### Output: format, length, tone

- No verbosity, prefill or tone controls are published. For speech tasks Google gives exact prompts: a transcription prompt that asks for the transcription only, with digits written as digits (write 1.7, not the spelled-out form), and a translation prompt that asks for the transcription, a newline, then the target-language label and translation [gemma-prompt-formatting-4, gemma-audio] (L).

### Tools and agents

- Same native tool tokens and history rule as the 31B [gemma-function-calling-4] (L). Tau2 (thinking mode, maker): airline 75.0, retail 77.6, telecom 54.4, average 69.0, slightly above the 26B A4B's 68.2 and below the 31B's 76.9. Terminal Bench Hard (harness not stated) is 18.0, above the 26B A4B's 14.0 and below the 31B's 36.0 [gemma-4-tech-report] (L).
- Google's launch post pitches it for multi-step reasoning and agentic workflows on local hardware and says its benchmark results approach the 26B A4B at less than half the total memory [gemma-12b-blog] (L). An independent multi-turn benchmark found it weak at a long interactive task (see Behaviour observed in practice) [arxiv-amigo] (M).

### Coding

- LiveCodeBench v6 72.0, Codeforces Elo 1659, SciCode 38.0 (thinking mode, maker) [gemma-4-tech-report] (L). No repository-level agent result from Google, and none from independent measurers: no row on Scale's SWE-Bench Pro V2 (page dated 2026-09-22), no readable Terminal-Bench 4.0 row, no METR horizon [scale-swe-pro-v2, tbench-leaderboard, metr-time-horizons] (M).

### Long context and retrieval

- Window 256K (262,144 tokens), sliding window 1024. In Google's long-context table, run without thinking: RULER 96.4 at 32k and 91.2 at 128k; LOFT 128k recall 66.4; GraphWalks F1 71.0; MTOB (English to Kalamang) 45.1 at about 128k and 41.9 at about 256k. MRCR v2 8-needle at 128k is 43.4 in the main table, in thinking mode [gemma-4-tech-report] (L). Its 128k scores are close to the 26B A4B's and below the 31B's.
- Memory: the report gives 24.0 GB bf16, 7.65 GB at Q4_0 and 0.28 GB for an int8 KV cache at 32k tokens, text only [gemma-4-tech-report] (L). Google's overview page gives 26.7 GB (BF16), 13.4 GB (SFP8) and 6.7 GB (Q4_0) to load the weights, with 20% overhead and no KV cache [gemma-core-docs] (L). Ollama lists its 12B download at 7.7 to 8.0 GB [ollama-gemma4] (A).

### Structured output

- Same position as the other sizes: the family launch post claims structured JSON output; no schema mode is documented [gemma-4-blog] (L). No 12B-specific test was found.

### Images, audio, other inputs

- Images: no vision encoder; 48 by 48 by 3 RGB patches go through a 35M-parameter projection with 2D position embeddings. The same token budgets (70 to 1120), image-before-text order and video limit (about 60 s at one frame per second) apply [gemma-4-tech-report, gemma-model-card-4] (L). This size loses more at a low budget than the others: at 1120 tokens MMMU Pro is 69.1, MATH-Vision 79.7, InfographicVQA 88.4 and OmniDocBench 1.5 distance 0.164; at 280 tokens 67.7, 76.7, 58.7 and 0.408 (lower distance is better). Document and infographic work is the case for a high budget [gemma-4-tech-report] (L).
- Audio: up to 30 seconds per clip, mono, 16 kHz, float32 samples scaled to the range -1 to 1, at 25 tokens per second of audio; the model card places audio after the text of the prompt [gemma-audio, gemma-model-card-4] (L). Reported FLEURS word error rates (character error for ko, ja): en 0.063, ko 0.057, ja 0.080, de 0.053, fr 0.081, es 0.038, it 0.030, pt-br 0.047, ru 0.068, ar 0.070; CoVoST translation BLEU into English: ja 26.4, de 41.9, fr 42.5, es 44.6, it 43.3, ru 50.5. Google presents this as competitive audio-text performance without an audio encoder [gemma-4-tech-report] (L). Chinese and Hindi are not in these tables. The model card instead gives single averages for the 12B, CoVoST 38.5 and FLEURS 0.069, both marked as excluding Chinese [gemma-model-card-4] (L).
- Ollama's model page carries an audio capability badge for the family, but every gemma4 tag in its list, the 12B included, shows text and image input only, so audio support may depend on the runtime and its version [ollama-gemma4] (A).

### Sampling and API parameters

- Temperature 1.0, top_p 0.95, top_k 64 [gemma-model-card-4] (L). The Gemini API lists no 12B; Google's pricing page shows Gemma 4 as free tier only. No output limit is published [gemma-on-gemini-api, gemini-pricing] (L).

### Migrating from the previous generation

- From Gemma 3 (12B or 27B): the 31B file lists the generation changes (system role, turn format, thinking, tool tokens, window 128K to 256K, image budgets, video, audio on this size). Gemma 3's 12B had a 128K window, text and image input and no audio [gemma-3-model-card] (L). Compared with the older Gemma 3n audio models, the 12B takes audio without an encoder, and Google's audio page gives a 25-tokens-per-second cost against 6.25 for Gemma 3n [gemma-audio] (L).

## What the system card reports

Google's model card and technical report cover all sizes together [gemma-model-card-4, gemma-4-tech-report] (L). The safety text is the same as for the 31B: five policy areas, filtered data, no-filter safety testing showing improvement over Gemma 3 and 3n and low unjustified refusals, and no published refusal rates, red-team, chemical, biological, radiological, nuclear, cyber, prompt-injection, sycophancy, reward-hacking or deception results (details in the [31B file](gemma-4-31b-it.md)). The 12B has no Arena Text entry in the report or on the live board read. Google's 12B launch post gives no safety statement [gemma-12b-blog] (L).

## Behaviour observed in practice

- Long interactive grounding (M, [arxiv-amigo]): in the AMIGO 20-question image game (thinking off), Gemma 4 12B reached 2.7 to 5.7% verified-correct accuracy across the five similarity thresholds, against 25 to 28% for the 31B at the lowest three thresholds. It used all 20 questions without a final guess in 52.6 to 56.5% of runs at every threshold (the 31B: under 8%) and had high rates of invalid questions after the upload phase (41.8 to 54.0% of post-upload turns penalised as invalid or redundant, against 9.6 to 13.0% for the 31B), while following the upload-phase rules well. Its macro-average recovery from one corrupted answer was 33.8%, against 59.1% for the 31B.
- Independent speed: Artificial Analysis measured 114.8 output tokens per second and 2.41 s to first token, through the one API provider it tracks for this model [aa-gemma-4-12b] (M).
- No practitioner reports specific to the 12B were found beyond launch coverage that repeats Google's numbers.

## Open questions

- Whether the encoder-free design explains the weaker interactive and low-budget vision results, or whether those are size effects; no ablation was found.
- Whether the 12B takes audio on each runtime (Ollama's tag list shows text and image only) and whether the Gemini API will serve the 12B (it is not listed there).
- Which audio figures to use: the model card's averages (CoVoST 38.5, FLEURS 0.069, excluding Chinese) or the report's per-language table, which gives no average.
- Whether the thinking-off thought-tag behaviour is identical across runtimes.
- A full Artificial Analysis evaluation of the 12B, which is still an estimate.
