# Gemma 4 E2B (instruction-tuned)

Gemma 4 E2B is the smallest model of the Gemma 4 generation (class Gemma, generation 4): 2.3 billion effective parameters (5.1 billion with per-layer embeddings), 35 layers, a 128K window, open weights under Apache 2.0, taking text, image, video and audio and returning text. Google released it on 2026-04-02 for phones, browsers and other edge devices (its overview page names Pixel and Chrome as examples); its technical report puts the text-only weights at 0.8 GB with Google's mobile quantization. Its predecessor in the edge line is Gemma 3n E2B. Google publishes one prompting and formatting guide for all Gemma 4 sizes; this file states what differs for the E2B and links to the [31B file](gemma-4-31b-it.md), which holds the family-wide rules.

## How to instruct it

Family-wide guidance, with the size table, is in [gemma-4-31b-it.md](gemma-4-31b-it.md). Differences for this model follow.

### Setting up the prompt

- Same system, user and model roles, turn markers and think token [gemma-prompt-formatting-4] (L). Instruction following (thinking mode, maker): IFEval 94.6 (Gemma 3 27B: 90.4) and IFBench 38.0 (Gemma 3 27B: 32.0) [gemma-4-tech-report] (L). Google's model card says the family does best on clearly framed tasks [gemma-model-card-4] (L); the E2B has the lowest scores of the family on every row of Google's main table that includes it (Humanity's Last Exam has no E2B entry) except Tau2 telecom, where it edges the E4B (19.7 against 18.4) (see Benchmarks).

### Reasoning and effort

- Same on or off switch and no levels. As with the E4B, the E2B template does not insert an empty thought channel when thinking is off, and the model card says only the larger models write an empty block in that case [gemma-thinking, gemma-model-card-4] (L).
- Google's LOW-thinking instruction and history-stripping rule apply [gemma-prompt-formatting-4] (L). The practitioner test of thinking on a Japanese commonsense set found accuracy loss at every size; the E2B scored 87.3% without it (A) [classmethod-dgx-spark].

### Output: format, length, tone

- No verbosity, prefill or tone controls are published. Google gives exact transcription and translation prompts for speech input [gemma-prompt-formatting-4, gemma-audio] (L).

### Tools and agents

- Same native tool tokens and history rule as the 31B [gemma-function-calling-4] (L). Tau2 (thinking mode, maker): airline 31.0, retail 34.6, telecom 19.7 in the technical report. The model card's single average is 24.5, but the three report rows average 28.4; no source explains the difference [gemma-model-card-4, gemma-4-tech-report] (L). Terminal Bench Hard (harness not stated): 3.0, against 36.0 for the 31B. Google's guidance does not single out this size for tool use; the numbers show it far behind the larger sizes on multi-turn tool tasks.

### Coding

- LiveCodeBench v6 44.0, Codeforces Elo 633, SciCode 21.0 (thinking mode, maker) [gemma-4-tech-report] (L). Google reports no repository-level agent result; no independent one exists: no row on Scale's SWE-Bench Pro V2 (page dated 2026-09-22), no readable Terminal-Bench 4.0 row, no METR horizon [scale-swe-pro-v2, tbench-leaderboard, metr-time-horizons] (M).

### Long context and retrieval

- Window 128K (131,072 tokens); sliding window 512; 4 local layers to 1 global layer (5 to 1 in the other sizes). In Google's long-context table, run without thinking: RULER 83.0 at 32k and 70.4 at 128k; LOFT 128k recall 50.5; GraphWalks F1 4.1 (the E4B scores 50.9 and Gemma 3 27B 32.8); MTOB (English to Kalamang) 15.4 and (reverse) 28.2 at about 128k. MRCR v2 8-needle at 128k is 19.1 in the main table, in thinking mode [gemma-4-tech-report] (L). RULER at 32k is high while GraphWalks is near zero, so scores differ a lot by task type.
- Memory: the report gives 4.6 GB bf16, 0.8 GB with Google's mobile quantization and 0.05 GB for an int8 KV cache at 32k tokens, text only. Google's quantization-aware-training post says the mobile format brings the E2B to 1 GB and the text-only E2B without per-layer embeddings under 1 GB [gemma-4-tech-report, gemma-qat-blog] (L). Google's overview page gives 11.4 GB (BF16), 5.7 GB (SFP8), 2.9 GB (Q4_0), 1.1 GB (mobile) and 0.84 GB (mobile, text only) to load the weights, with 20% overhead and no KV cache [gemma-core-docs] (L). Ollama lists the E2B download at 4.6 to 7.5 GB [ollama-gemma4] (A).

### Structured output

- Family position: the launch post claims structured JSON output; no schema mode is documented [gemma-4-blog] (L). A practitioner's ten-image JSON tagging test parsed every time for every size, E2B included (small sample) [classmethod-dgx-spark] (A).

### Images, audio, other inputs

- Images: 150M vision encoder; token budgets 70 to 1120; image before text. At 1120 tokens: MMMU Pro 44.2, MATH-Vision 52.4, InfographicVQA 63.9, OmniDocBench 1.5 distance 0.290; at 280 tokens: 43.2, 53.0, 44.6 and 0.496. Document-style tasks fall most at a low budget [gemma-4-tech-report] (L).
- Audio: 305M encoder, 87 MB on disk after quantization; up to 30 seconds, mono 16 kHz, 25 tokens per second; the model card places audio after the text of the prompt. The technical report gives averages of 0.090 FLEURS error over 12 languages and 35.4 CoVoST BLEU into English over seven source languages; Chinese has the highest error (character error 0.187) and the lowest translation score (17.9) [gemma-audio, gemma-4-tech-report] (L). The model card lists different single values for the E2B, CoVoST 33.47 and FLEURS 0.09, and neither source explains the gap [gemma-model-card-4] (L).
- Video: up to about 60 seconds at one frame per second [gemma-model-card-4] (L).

### Sampling and API parameters

- Temperature 1.0, top_p 0.95, top_k 64 [gemma-model-card-4] (L). The Gemini API model list read names only the 31B and 26B A4B; Google's pricing page shows Gemma 4 as free tier only. No output limit is published [gemma-on-gemini-api, gemini-pricing] (L).

### Migrating from the previous generation

- From Gemma 3n E2B (the earlier edge model) or Gemma 3: the 31B file lists the generation changes. For the edge line, Google reports that the audio encoder fell from 680M to 305M parameters and from 390 MB to 87 MB on disk, while translation improved by 12% and transcription by 17% in relative terms for the E2B (CoVoST 35.4 against 31.6 for Gemma 3n E2B; FLEURS 0.090 against 0.108). Audio now costs 25 tokens per second against 6.25 for Gemma 3n [gemma-4-tech-report, gemma-audio] (L). Google says the E2B roughly matches Gemma 3 27B's benchmark performance with a tenth of the parameters; that holds on some rows (GPQA 43.4 against 42.4, IFEval 94.6 against 90.4, OmniDocBench distance 0.290 against 0.365 where lower is better) and not on others (MMLU Pro 60.0 against 67.6, MMMU Pro 44.2 against 49.7) [gemma-4-tech-report] (L).

## What the system card reports

Google's model card and technical report cover all sizes together [gemma-model-card-4, gemma-4-tech-report] (L). The safety text is the same as for the 31B: five policy areas, filtered data, no-filter safety testing showing improvement over Gemma 3 and 3n and low unjustified refusals, and no published refusal rates, red-team, chemical, biological, radiological, nuclear, cyber, prompt-injection, sycophancy, reward-hacking or deception results (details in the [31B file](gemma-4-31b-it.md)). The report gives no Arena Text entry for this size.

## Behaviour observed in practice

- One practitioner's DGX Spark run with Ollama (2026-04-03, BF16): 0.22 s per question and 87.3% on a 1,119-question Japanese commonsense set; with thinking on, accuracy fell to 85.6% and answers took about 31 times longer (A, one setup) [classmethod-dgx-spark].
- Artificial Analysis shows no output-speed figure for this model and a price of $0.00 [aa-gemma-4-e2b] (M).
- No other practitioner reports specific to the E2B were found.

## Open questions

- Which Tau2 average for the E2B is right (24.5 on the model card, 28.4 from the report's rows), and which audio averages (the report's 35.4 and 0.090, or the model card's 33.47 and 0.09).
- Why GraphWalks drops to 4.1 for this size when RULER at 32k is 83.0, and whether prompt format can help.
- How the E2B performs on agentic tasks; no source reports one.
- A full Artificial Analysis evaluation, since its score is an estimate.
