# Dramabox

Dramabox is an open-weight expressive speech synthesis model of Resemble AI, class Dramabox, generation 1, released on 2026-04-17. It is a 3.3B audio-only diffusion transformer, adapted from Lightricks' LTX-2.3 audio branch and conditioned on text embeddings from a 12B Gemma model. It is a voice actor model in the full sense: the prompt is a short scene, and the model reads the stage directions as performance cues and speaks only the quoted lines. It directs emotion, delivery, laughs, sighs, breaths, pauses and transitions in plain English. A voice reference is optional. Maker-wide facts are in the [Resemble AI README](README.md).

## How to instruct it

### Setting up the prompt

- **Scene format.** The prompt is a short scene. Spoken lines go inside double quotation marks. Everything outside the quotes is a stage direction that the model reads as a cue and does not speak [hf-dramabox].
- **Stage directions.** The card's examples: She sighs deeply; He clears his throat; A long pause; Her voice cracks; He gulps nervously. Longer directions set the voice and manner. One example is a regal woman speaking with cold fury in a measured, low voice. Another is a calm, authoritative football commentator with a smooth, measured delivery [hf-dramabox].
- **Sounds inside quotes.** The card says laughter and similar sounds are written as one word with no separators: Hahaha, Hehehe, Mmmmm, Ugh, Argh, Hmm. Words such as Sigh, Gasp, Cough, Ahem and Pfft inside quotes are spoken literally, so the card puts those sounds in stage directions [hf-dramabox].
- **Sample prompts.** The card shows a queen who drops from cold fury to a venomous whisper, a giggling girl, a sinister villain with a soft chuckle, a talk-show host wheezing with laughter, a named football commentator with crowd sound, and a boy band singing in harmony. It shows that crowd and music cues can sit in the same prompt [hf-dramabox].
- **Voice.** A clip of 10 seconds or more fixes the timbre. Without it the model chooses a voice that fits the scene [hf-dramabox].

None.

### Output: format, length, tone

- **Guidance settings.** `cfg_scale` (default 2.5) sets how strictly the output follows the prompt: lower is more natural, higher more faithful and more dramatic. `stg_scale` (default 1.5) adds expressive emphasis without the saturation of a higher guidance value [hf-dramabox].
- **Duration.** The model estimates it from the prompt with a 10 percent margin (`duration_multiplier` 1.1). You can also set it in seconds. The card suggests 20 to 60 seconds for music or long scenes [hf-dramabox].
- **Reference length.** The model conditions on 3 to 30 seconds of the clip, default 10 [hf-dramabox].
- **Watermark.** The Perth watermark is on by default [hf-dramabox].

### Voice: turn-taking, interruption and speech style

Turn-taking does not apply. A prompt can hold several lines, but the model does not manage a live conversation.

**How to direct delivery.** The prompt describes the scene in prose. The prompt is the whole control: speaker identity, emotion, delivery, laughs, sighs, breaths, pauses and transitions [hf-dramabox].

**Strength.** The card gives no score. It shows six sample prompts with reference and generated audio [hf-dramabox].

**What fails.** Words such as Sigh or Cough inside quotes are spoken. High guidance makes speech more dramatic. The card gives no other failure list [hf-dramabox].

**Voice design and cloning.** Description-driven voice choice and optional cloning from a clip.

**Multi-speaker dialogue.** The card's samples include lines by several voices, such as a boy band, in one prompt. The card does not give a dialogue syntax [hf-dramabox].

**Long-form consistency.** Not documented.

**Pronunciation and normalisation.** Not documented.

**Speed.** About 2.5 seconds per generation on an H100 with a warm server [hf-dramabox].
