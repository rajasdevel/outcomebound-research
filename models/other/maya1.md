# Maya1

Maya1 is an open-weight speech synthesis model of the speech start-up Maya Research, class Maya, generation 1. It has about 3B parameters, speaks English, makes a voice from a written description and adds 17 inline emotion and sound tags. It is a voice actor model. Maya Research released it on 2025-10-18 under Apache-2.0 and updated the weights page in July 2026. The company's newer Maya 2 models are hosted only; its site lists them as hosted and lists Maya1 and Veena as the open models. Maker-specific facts are in this file.

## How to instruct it

### Setting up the prompt

- **Description plus text.** The prompt builder puts a description wrapper in front of the text: the wrapper holds the voice description, then comes the line to speak. The description is natural language [hf-maya1].
- **What a description holds.** The card's examples give gender, age, accent, role, pitch, timbre, pacing, tone and intensity. One is a woman in her 30s with an American accent, an event host, energetic, with clear diction. Another is a dark villain: a man in his 40s with a British accent, low pitch, gravelly timbre, slow pacing and an angry tone at high intensity. A third is a demon with a screaming tone at high intensity [hf-maya1].
- **Inline tags.** A tag in angle brackets goes at the point where it should sound. The emotions file lists 17: laugh, laugh_harder, sigh, chuckle, gasp, angry, excited, whisper, cry, scream, sing, snort, exhale, gulp, giggle, sarcastic and curious. The card names eight and says there are 12 or more besides; its comparison table says 20+ [hf-maya1] [hf-maya1-emotions].
- **Sampling that the example uses.** Temperature 0.4, top-p 0.9, repetition penalty 1.1 and up to 2,048 new tokens, with generation ending at the speech-end token [hf-maya1].

### Voice: turn-taking, interruption and speech style

Turn-taking does not apply.

**How to direct delivery.** Two levers. The description sets the voice and its general manner (accent, pitch, timbre, pace, tone and intensity). Inline tags set events and emotions at a place in the sentence [hf-maya1].

**Strength.** The card's comparison table is a feature list against ElevenLabs, OpenAI TTS and Coqui TTS, with no measured scores. Artificial Analysis lists it at Elo 1046.47 (page headline; 1048.58 in the page data) in its open-weight set [hf-maya1] [aa-maya1].

**What fails.** The card lists none.

**Voice design and cloning.** Voice design is the main feature. Cloning is not documented.

**Multi-speaker dialogue.** Not documented.

**Long-form consistency.** Not documented. Without cloning, the same description may not give an identical voice on each run (inferred; the card does not say).

**Pronunciation and normalisation.** Not documented.

**Latency.** Maya Research claims sub-100 ms with vLLM and no source test [hf-maya1].

### Sampling and API parameters

The card's example uses `temperature` 0.4, `top_p` 0.9, `repetition_penalty` 1.1, `max_new_tokens` 2048 and `min_new_tokens` 28 [hf-maya1].

### Migrating from the previous generation

Maya Research's earlier open model, Veena (2025-06-24), is a Hindi and English voice model that the company calls its first. The pages read give it no tags or description control, so it has no file. Maya 2 Native, Global and Flash are hosted models. The company's site lists three hosted models and two open ones, Maya1 and Veena, and no Maya 2 weights were found on Hugging Face [maya-site].
