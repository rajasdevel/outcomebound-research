# MOSS-VoiceGenerator

MOSS-VoiceGenerator is an open-weight voice design model in the MOSS-TTS family of the OpenMOSS team and MOSI.AI. It has 1.7B parameters. A written instruction and the text to speak go in together, and the model makes a voice with the described timbre, emotion, pace and pitch. It is a voice actor model and needs no reference audio. It was released on 2026-02-08 under Apache-2.0. The other MOSS-TTS models take pause and duration markers and pronunciation control but no emotion or style direction in their cards, so they have no file. Maker-specific facts are in this file.

## How to instruct it

### Setting up the prompt

- **Two required fields.** The user message has `text`, the words to speak in Chinese or English, and `instruction`, which sets the style of the speech. The card says the instruction can carry detailed style directions such as emotion, speed, pitch and voice characteristics [hf-mossvg].
- **How it works.** The instruction and the text are joined and tokenised together. The model learned to align instructions with acoustic features, so one input sets timbre, style and content [hf-mossvg].
- **Examples in our words.** A tired, hoarse older voice grumbling slowly with a slight groan (Chinese); an enthusiastic cooking-show host with lively intonation (Chinese); a cheerful innkeeper who speaks loudly in American English, a little rough but warm; a clear, neutral voice for phonetic practice with even tempo and precise articulation [hf-mossvg].
- **Decoding settings.** The card recommends audio temperature 1.5, top-p 0.6, top-k 50 and repetition penalty 1.1. It says a higher temperature adds variation and a lower one steadies the prosody [hf-mossvg].

### Coding

The card gives a Transformers example with a processor, batch conversations and a wav writer. It sets cuDNN scaled-dot-product attention off because the card calls that backend broken, and prefers FlashAttention 2 where the GPU allows it [hf-mossvg].

Text and an instruction. There is no reference audio input.

### Voice: turn-taking, interruption and speech style

Turn-taking does not apply.

**How to direct delivery.** One free-form instruction per line of text. It names emotion, speed, pitch and voice character [hf-mossvg].

**Strength.** The card gives no score for instruction following. It claims highly expressive emotional delivery without a test [hf-mossvg].

**What fails.** The card lists none. It gives recommended decoding values and says how temperature changes the prosody [hf-mossvg].

**Voice design and cloning.** Voice design only. The maker positions the model as a design layer for the other MOSS-TTS models, which clone [hf-mossvg].

**Multi-speaker dialogue.** Not in this model. MOSS-TTSD-v1.0 is the dialogue model of the family [hf-moss-ttsd].

**Long-form consistency, pronunciation.** Not documented for this model. MOSS-TTS v1.5 adds an explicit pause marker with a duration in seconds, for example a marker for 3.2 seconds [hf-moss-ttsv15].

### Sampling and API parameters

`audio_temperature` 1.5, `audio_top_p` 0.6, `audio_top_k` 50 and `audio_repetition_penalty` 1.1 are the recommended values [hf-mossvg].
