# VoxCPM2

VoxCPM2 is an open-weight speech synthesis model of OpenBMB, class VoxCPM, generation 2. It has about 2B parameters, outputs 48 kHz audio, and covers 30 languages. It makes a voice from a description in parentheses and steers a cloned voice with a style note in the same place. It is a voice actor model. OpenBMB released it in April 2026 under Apache-2.0, free for commercial use. The earlier VoxCPM1.5 has no voice design or style control in the maker's version table, so it is not a voice actor model and has no file here.

## How to instruct it

### Setting up the prompt

- **Parentheses at the start of the text.** To design a voice, put the description in parentheses before the line. Example: a young woman, gentle and sweet voice, then the sentence to speak. To steer a cloned voice, put a style note in the same place, for example slightly faster, cheerful tone, and pass a reference clip [hf-voxcpm2].
- **Guidance.** The examples pass a classifier-free guidance value of 2.0 and 10 inference timesteps [hf-voxcpm2].
- **No tags for sounds.** The card names no inline sound tags. It says the model infers prosody and expressiveness from the text content [hf-voxcpm2].
- **Cloning modes.** Basic cloning needs only a clip. Ultimate cloning needs the clip and its exact transcript, passed as both reference and prompt, and continues the audio for the best likeness [hf-voxcpm2].
- **Run it more than once.** The card says voice design and style control vary between runs. It advises generating one to three times to get the wanted result [hf-voxcpm2].

### Reasoning and effort

None.

### Output: format, length, tone

- **Audio.** 48 kHz output from a 16 kHz reference, with no separate upsampler. Streaming is available through `generate_streaming` [hf-voxcpm2].
- **Length.** The card says very long or highly expressive input can be unstable, and the maximum sequence is 8,192 tokens [hf-voxcpm2].
- **Tone.** Parenthesis notes and the text itself set it.

### Tools and agents

Not applicable.

### Coding

The `voxcpm` package installs with pip. Fine-tuning supports full and LoRA training with 5 to 10 minutes of audio. Nano-vLLM serves it in production and llama.cpp-omni runs it on device [hf-voxcpm2] [gh-voxcpm].

### Long context and retrieval

Not applicable. The card warns about instability on very long input [hf-voxcpm2].

### Structured output

Not applicable.

### Images, audio, other inputs

Text, an optional parenthesis note, and an optional reference clip with its transcript.

### Voice: turn-taking, interruption and speech style

Turn-taking does not apply.

**How to direct delivery.** The direction is a short description or style note in parentheses at the head of the text. The card gives three kinds of note: a voice description (gender, age, tone, emotion, pace), a style note for a cloned voice (emotion, pace, expression) and none, where the model infers delivery from the text. The notes are free text; the card lists no closed vocabulary [hf-voxcpm2].

**Strength.** On InstructTTSEval (voice design), OpenBMB reports attribute scores of 85.2 (Chinese) and 84.2 (English). It is ahead of Qwen3-TTS-1.7B-VD on all three English scores. It is level with it on Chinese attribute perception and behind it on the Chinese description and response scores. BreezeBlue runs benchmarks as a competing maker. They rank the model last of six on voice direction (2.49 of 5) and last of seven on voice design role fit [gh-voxcpm] [bb-blog2v].

**What fails.** Results vary between runs for design and style control; very long or highly expressive input can be unstable; performance differs by language with training data [hf-voxcpm2].

**Voice design and cloning.** Both are in one model, as above.

**Multi-speaker dialogue.** Not documented.

**Long-form consistency.** Not documented beyond the warning above.

**Pronunciation and normalisation.** No phoneme or lexicon feature is documented on the pages read.

**Speed.** The real-time factor is about 0.30 on an RTX 4090 and about 0.13 with Nano-vLLM [hf-voxcpm2].

### Sampling and API parameters

Python: `cfg_value`, `inference_timesteps`, `reference_wav_path`, `prompt_wav_path`, `prompt_text`, and a `load_denoiser` flag [hf-voxcpm2].

### Migrating from the previous generation

VoxCPM1.5 (0.6B, 44.1 kHz, Chinese and English) uses continuation cloning only. It has no voice design and no controllable cloning, so parenthesis notes do nothing there. VoxCPM2 adds both, 30 languages, 48 kHz output and isolated-reference cloning [gh-voxcpm].

## What the system card reports

OpenBMB publishes no system card. The card's limitations section forbids impersonation, fraud and disinformation, and asks that AI-generated content be labelled [hf-voxcpm2].

## Behaviour observed in practice

- **Competing maker's benchmark (L, third-party vendor).** BreezeBlue ranks it last in both its voice design and voice direction tables [bb-blog2v].
- No practitioner report was read.

## Benchmarks
